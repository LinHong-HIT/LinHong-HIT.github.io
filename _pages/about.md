Lin Hong is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), collaborating with [Prof. Fumin Zhang](https://fumin-home.hkust.edu.hk/). Lin Hong obtained his Ph.D. degree in Robotic Engineering from the Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen), under the supervision of [Prof. Xin Wang](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/wangxin). From 2023 to 2024, he was at the Chair of Robotics, AI, and Real-Time Systems at the Technical University of Munich, 
working under the guidance of [Prof. Alois Knoll](https://www.professoren.tum.de/en/knoll-alois-christian/). Lin Hong's research interests are centered on marine robotics, robotic version, and deep reinforcement learning.
